How it goes A typical engagement, start to finish.

The shape of an engagement

01

Conversation

A free call, usually 45 minutes. You describe the situation; I ask the questions that tend to surface what's underneath it. If I'm not the right fit, I'll say so and point you somewhere better.

02

Scope and proposal

A written proposal with a fixed price, a defined end date, and a plain description of what you'll have when we're done. No hourly surprises.

03

The work

Weekly check-ins, visible progress, and direct access to me — not a junior associate you've never met.

04

Handoff

You keep everything: the documents, the process, the reasoning behind each decision. The goal is that you don't need me again for the same problem.
