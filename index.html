The problem we solve

Three symptoms, one root cause

A department can't explain why its costs moved. Supplies run short in one unit while the same item expires in another. New hires take six months to become useful, and the person training them is carrying a full workload at the same time.

These look like separate problems. They rarely are. Underneath all three is usually the same gap: nobody documented how the work is actually supposed to happen, so there's no reliable record of it, no basis for planning, and nothing to teach from.

We start by making that invisible process visible — then builds the measurement, supply discipline, and training material out of what we find.

The number you need usually already exists. It's just sitting in three spreadsheets, two systems, and one person's memory.

  • Findings documented in writing, not delivered as a slide deck
  • Procedures your team can run after the engagement ends
  • Named internal owner trained before handoff
  • No recommendation that requires us to stay

Our approach

How an engagement works

Every project follows the same four stages, scaled to the size of the problem.

STAGE 01

Initial call

An initial call to understand the situation. If we are not the right fit, we will say so.

STAGE 02

Scope & proposal

A written proposal with fixed pricing, a defined end date, and a clear description of deliverables.

STAGE 03

Assessment & build

Time observing the actual work — the stockroom, the unit, the receiving dock — then building the fix.

STAGE 04

Handoff

Documentation, templates, and a trained internal owner so the improvement holds after we leave.
