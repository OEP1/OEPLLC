Approach

What I believe about this work

Consulting has a reputation problem, and it earned it. Too many engagements end with a document nobody reads and a team that feels judged rather than helped.

I try to work the other way. I spend the first weeks listening to the people who actually do the job, because they usually know what's broken and have been saying so for a while. My value isn't knowing more than your team — it's having the outside standing to say the thing out loud, and the time to see it through.

I also try to leave. A good engagement ends with your people capable of running the thing without me.
